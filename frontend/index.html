You get a 0–100 AI Visibility Score, a letter grade, a category breakdown, and a prioritized fix list.

What is Generative Engine Optimization?

GEO is the practice of making a website legible and attractive to the retrieval and citation layer of large language models. Unlike traditional SEO — which optimizes for a keyword-based ranking algorithm — GEO optimizes for two things: AI crawl access and quotability by LLMs.

  • Crawl access — explicit allow/deny rules for GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, and 12 other AI bots in robots.txt; machine-readable manifests in .well-known/; a complete sitemap.xml; the llms.txt convention.
  • Quotability — semantic HTML, clean heading structure, JSON-LD structured data, canonical URLs, authorship signals, and content shapes LLMs prefer to cite (definitions, lists, comparison tables, step-by-step instructions, FAQ blocks, quotable statistics).

What Vigilath checks

Each audit runs through 23 categories grouped into five themes:

  1. Crawl access — HTTPS, robots.txt, llms.txt, sitemap.xml, .well-known manifests, IndexNow.
  2. Content extractability — SSR vs. client-rendered, content-to-HTML ratio, semantic tags, heading structure, internal links.
  3. Meta & structured data — title, description, canonical, Open Graph, Twitter card, JSON-LD (Organization, WebSite, FAQ, HowTo, Breadcrumbs).
  4. Authority & trust — security headers, author attribution, sameAs profile links, cross-platform presence.
  5. Answer-format readiness — definitions, step-by-step, comparison tables, pros/cons, TL;DR, FAQs, quotable stats.
